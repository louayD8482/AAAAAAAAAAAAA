🛡️ 1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

At Noor Al-Islam ("نور الإسلام"), we strictly respect and safeguard your privacy. This application is an Islamic utility app designed to provide Holy Quran recitation, daily Adhkar, accurate prayer times, Qibla compass, and an AI Islamic Companion, maintaining the highest standards of transparency and user safety.

🔒 2. Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

We do NOT collect, store, sell, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data. Specifically:

  • No names, phone numbers, addresses, or emails are requested or recorded.
  • No user account or login is required to use any part of the application.
  • No behavioral tracking, analytics profiling, or user fingerprinting is conducted.
  • No tracking cookies, third-party analytics SDKs, or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) are used.

📍 3. Device Permissions & Local Processing

The application only requests minimal system permissions strictly necessary to deliver its religious utilities:

  • Location Permission: Used solely on-device to compute astronomical prayer times and determine the Qibla compass bearing. Your location coordinates are never saved to external servers or shared with any third party.
  • Local Notifications: Used solely to schedule local device reminders for Prayer Times, Adhan calls, and morning/evening Adhkar directly through iOS Local Notifications API without requiring persistent external server connections.

🤖 4. AI Features & Third-Party Processing (Smart Islamic Assistant & Tafsir)

The app includes an optional AI-powered Islamic Advisor and Quranic Tafsir feature:

  • When a user submits a religious query or requests Tafsir for a specific Surah/Ayah, the text prompt is sent securely and encrypted over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API solely to generate the relevant scholarly text response.
  • Data Transmitted: Only the text query entered by the user is transmitted. No personal identifiers, device identifiers, user accounts, or location coordinates are attached or shared.
  • No Commercial Use: Query data is processed strictly to return the immediate answer and is not used for advertising or marketing profiling.

🚫 5. Advertising & Tracking Free

Noor Al-Islam is 100% ad-free. We do not incorporate commercial advertising SDKs, ad networks, or data brokers.

👶 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Noor Al-Islam is safe for users of all ages, including children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Family & Privacy guidelines.
