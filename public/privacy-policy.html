🛡️ 1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

At Noor Al-Islam ("نور الإسلام"), we strictly respect and safeguard user privacy. This application is an Islamic utility app providing Holy Quran recitation, daily Adhkar, accurate prayer times, Qibla direction, and an AI Islamic Companion, fully compliant with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1).

📍 2. Location Data Usage & Purpose

The app requests Location permission (Precise or Coarse GPS) solely for the following explicit features:

  • Astronomical Prayer Times: Your geographic coordinates are used to calculate accurate prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) based on approved astronomical calculation methods.
  • Qibla Compass Direction: Coordinates are used to calculate the exact azimuth bearing pointing towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah.
  • No Tracking & No Storage: Location coordinates are processed solely for immediate calculation and are not permanently logged, recorded on external databases, or shared with third parties.

🤖 3. AI Features & User Query Processing

The app features optional AI Islamic assistance and Quranic Tafsir capabilities:

  • User Text Inquiries: When a user submits an Islamic question or requests Tafsir for a specific Ayah/Surah, the entered text is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to our processing backend and Google Gemini API solely to generate the textual response.
  • Zero Personal Identifiers: No personal details, names, emails, device IDs, or location coordinates are attached to or transmitted with AI requests.
  • No Commercial Profiling: Text queries are processed in real-time to return the answer and are never used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
