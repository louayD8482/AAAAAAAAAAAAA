🛡️ 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At Noor Al-Islam ("نور الإسلام"), we strictly respect and protect your privacy. This application is an Islamic utility app designed to provide Holy Quran recitation, daily Adhkar, accurate prayer times, and Qibla direction without collecting or tracking any personal user information.

🔒 2. Zero Data Collection Policy

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data. Specifically:

  • No names, phone numbers, or email addresses are requested or collected.
  • No user registration, accounts, or logins are required to use any feature of the app.
  • No behavioral analytics, profiling, or cross-device tracking is implemented.
  • No third-party tracking cookies or identifier for advertisers (IDFA) are used.

📍 3. Device Permissions & 100% On-Device Processing

The application only requests minimal system permissions strictly necessary to execute its primary Islamic features, and all processing is handled 100% locally on your iOS device:

  • Location Permission: Used exclusively on-device to compute astronomical prayer times and determine the Qibla compass bearing. Your location coordinates are never transmitted to or saved on external servers.
  • Local Notifications: Used solely to schedule local device reminders for Prayer Times, Adhan calls, and morning/evening Adhkar directly through iOS Local Notifications API.

🚫 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, commercial trackers, or marketing analytics platforms.

👶 5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Noor Al-Islam is safe for users of all ages, including children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Family & Privacy guidelines.
